Add your teacher to your repository

Your teacher needs to see your pydle work. It takes about a minute.

Add this GitHub account as a collaborator: your teacher

  1. Sign in to github.com.
  2. Open github.com/your-username/pydle-workspace. That is the repository pydle made for you.
  3. Click Settings across the top of the repository. If you cannot see it, you are not signed in as the owner.
  4. In the sidebar on the left, click Collaborators.
  5. Click Add people.
  6. Type your teacher's username and pick the right account from the list.
  7. Click Add to send the invitation.

The invitation has to be accepted. GitHub emails the invitation, and your teacher has to click the link in it before the repository appears for them. If it does not arrive, check the username is spelled correctly and ask them to look in junk.

If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
There is no Settings tabYou are not signed in as the owner of the repository, or you are looking at someone else's. Sign in and open your own pydle-workspace.
Add people is greyed outGitHub asks for your password again, or for a two-factor code, before changing access. Complete that and try again.
The username is not foundGitHub usernames contain no spaces. Check the spelling with your teacher.
Your teacher cannot see the repositoryThey have not accepted the invitation yet. Send them the email again or ask them to open the link.
